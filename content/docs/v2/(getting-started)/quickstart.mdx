---
title: "Start selling"
description: "Create your account, set up a Workspace, and accept payments."
---

Set up your account and Workspace, then share your storefront or connect the Checkout API. See [Pricing](/v2/pricing) to compare Merchant of record and Pandabase Direct.

<Callout>
In this version of Pandabase, Workspaces replace stores as the main containers for your business. Each Workspace is isolated and can have several stores attached to it. Your existing stores continue to work as usual.
</Callout>

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Create your account

    Sign up at [merchant.pandabase.io](https://merchant.pandabase.io/auth/signup) with your email address. We'll send you a magic link to verify your account.
  </Step>

  <Step>
    ### Complete your profile

    Open the magic link and enter your username, first and last name, phone number, and country. Review and accept the legal policies to continue.
  </Step>

  <Step>
    ### Create your Workspace

    Enter your Workspace name, URL, statement descriptor, support email, and country of operation. Choose a category and describe your business and what you sell. Your storefront goes live when you submit the form.

  <Step>
    ### Verify your identity

    Submit your ID and verify your phone number with two-factor authentication (2FA). You can start accepting payments before verification, but you need to complete it before we release payouts.

    <Callout>
    We use Sumsub to verify your identity before sending payouts. Sumsub processes your ID; Pandabase doesn't store it.
    </Callout>
  </Step>

  <Step>
    ### Complete the Workspace questionnaire

    Open the questionnaire from the banner at the top of your dashboard and answer the questions about your business.
  </Step>

  <Step>
    ### Accept your first payment

    Share your storefront link or connect the [Checkout API](/developers/learn/hosted-checkout). The API guide is in the current documentation.

    With Merchant of record, Pandabase handles tax, disputes, and payouts. With Pandabase Direct, you remain the legal seller and keep responsibility for tax, disputes, and fraud liability.
  </Step>
