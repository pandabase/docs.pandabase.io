### Merchant of record

**5.9% + 50¢** per successful transaction.

- **+1.5%** for cards and alternative payment methods outside the US
- **+0.5%** for subscription payments

Included:

- Pandabase is the legal seller of record
- Sales tax, VAT, and GST filed and paid
- Fraud screening and dispute handling
- Processor fees
- Checkout, billing, and invoicing

### Pandabase Direct

**3.9% + 29¢** per successful transaction, plus **Interchange++** fees from our payment processing partner, passed through at cost.

Included:

- Checkout, billing, and invoicing
- The same payment methods and currencies as Merchant of record

Your responsibilities:

- Tax registration and filing
- Dispute and fraud liability

### Custom

Contact sales if you process a high volume of payments or need different rates. Custom plans can include:

- Volume discounts
- Multi-product pricing
- Country-specific rates
- Dedicated support

## Compare plans

The main difference is who acts as the legal seller. With Merchant of record, Pandabase handles tax and disputes. With Pandabase Direct, those responsibilities stay with you.

| Feature                                   | Pandabase Direct | Merchant of record |
| ----------------------------------------- | ---------------- | ------------------ |
| Checkout, cards, wallets, and local methods | Included       | Included           |
| Billing, invoicing, and payment links     | Included         | Included           |
| Legal seller of record                    | You              | Pandabase          |
| Sales tax and VAT registration            | You register     | Included           |
| Tax filing and remittance                 | You file         | Included           |
| Dispute handling and liability            | You              | Included           |
| Billing support for buyers                | Included         | Included           |
| Processor fees                            | Passed through   | Included           |

## Payments

| Product                                 | Plan               | Rate                                  |
| --------------------------------------- | ------------------ | ------------------------------------- |
| Cards and wallets                       | Merchant of record | 5.9% + 50¢ per successful transaction |
| Bank and local payment methods          | Merchant of record | 5.9% + 50¢ per successful transaction |
| Adaptive currency                       | Merchant of record | 3–4%, paid by the customer, not you   |
| Cards, wallets, and local methods       | Pandabase Direct   | 3.9% + 29¢ + Interchange++            |
| Standalone 3DS                          | Both               | $0.12 per authentication              |
| Hosted, embedded, and in-app checkout   | Both               | Included in the standard rate         |

- **Cards and wallets:** Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, Apple Pay, and Google Pay, online and in-app.
- **Bank and local payment methods:** iDEAL, Pix, UPI, Pay by Bank, Alipay, WeChat Pay, and more.
- **Adaptive currency:** show prices in the buyer's local currency at checkout.
- **Standalone 3DS:** ask the cardholder's bank to verify a card payment before you charge it, so eligible fraud liability shifts to the issuer.

With Merchant of record, cards and alternative payment methods from outside the US add **1.5%**, and subscription payments add **0.5%**.

## Billing and disputes

| Product                                 | Rate                                                      |
| --------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Recurring and usage-based billing       | +0.5% per subscription payment, on top of the transaction rate |
| Invoices                                | Included in the standard rate                             |
| Dispute handling                        | $30 per dispute                                           |
| Dispute prevention                      | $30 per dispute prevented                                 |

- **Recurring and usage-based billing:** plans, seats, credits, trials, proration, and failed payment retries.
- **Invoices:** hosted invoice pages with automatic reminders and receipts.
- **Dispute handling:** we respond to chargebacks with evidence on your behalf.
- **Dispute prevention:** resolve disputes with the card issuer before they become chargebacks.

## Example payment

A customer in the US pays **$49.00** for a one-time purchase, sold with Pandabase as merchant of record:

|                                  | Amount     |
| -------------------------------- | ---------- |
| Sale                             | $49.00     |
| Pandabase fee (5.9% × $49.00 + 50¢) | -$3.39  |
| **You keep**                     | **$45.61** |

You keep **$45.61** after the transaction fee. Payout fees and any other applicable charges are separate.
