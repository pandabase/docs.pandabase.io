Pandabase helps you accept payments for your digital products. We rebuilt this version from the ground up to support payments across multiple processors and give you more flexibility.

<Callout>
In this version of Pandabase, Workspaces replace stores as the main containers for your business. Each Workspace is isolated and can have several stores attached to it. Your existing stores continue to work as usual.
</Callout>

## Get started

Follow [Start selling](/v2/quickstart) to create your account and set up a Workspace. You can sell in two modes. See [Pricing](/v2/pricing) for rates and what each mode includes.

<Cards>
  <Card title="Merchant of record" icon={<Rocket />} href="/v2/merchant-of-record">
    Pandabase is the legal seller and handles payments, tax, disputes, and payouts for you.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Pandabase Direct" icon={<CreditCard />} href="/v2/direct">
    You remain the legal seller. Payments run through our processing partners, and you keep responsibility for tax, disputes, and fraud liability.
  </Card>
</Cards>
